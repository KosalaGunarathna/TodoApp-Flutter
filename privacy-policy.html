Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Todo App handles information when you use the application.

Information Stored Locally

Todo App stores your tasks, notes, dates, times, and app settings locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to our servers and is not shared by us with third parties.

Advertising

Todo App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect or use device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, and information related to advertising and app usage.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Todo App may schedule notifications on your device. Notification data is handled locally by the application.

Children's Privacy

Todo App does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The application does not require an account, name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored by the application. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
